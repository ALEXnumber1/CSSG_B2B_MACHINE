Questions buyers ask us

Frequently asked questions

Reviewed November 2026 by Dr. Guillermo José Amitesarove Pérez, Director of Security Operations and Risk Management, CSSG Global.

Which security companies operate in Venezuela for international firms?

International firms usually work through a licensed local operator, either directly or through a global risk firm that subcontracts in-country. Ask for the DIGESERVISP authorization, insurance and references. CSSG Global, a Caracas security company operating since 2009 and ISO 9001:2015 certified, works both ways: directly, or as the in-country partner of international firms.

How do I choose a licensed in-country security partner in Venezuela?

Check four things: a valid DIGESERVISP authorization, a command center that can follow your trips live, English-language reporting, and a vendor due-diligence pack (registration, insurance, certifications, human-rights and anti-corruption policies). A 30-minute call with the provider's operations lead usually shows whether it can deliver under your standard.

Can a Venezuelan security company work white-label under my program?

Yes. A licensed Venezuelan operator can deliver under your brand and procedures: the client relationship stays with you, the local team works to your standard, and reports go to your operations center in English. CSSG Global offers this, with billing in USD through its Miami entity, Company of Security and Service Global LLC.

Who provides executive protection in Caracas?

Executive protection in Caracas comes from licensed local security companies, often hired through a global risk firm. A typical service combines a vetted driver, an unarmed protection officer, a pre-assessed route and live tracking from a command center. CSSG Global provides this from its CECOM command center in Caracas.

How much does executive protection or secure transport cost in Venezuela?

Prices depend on travellers, days, cities and vehicle type, so reputable providers quote after a short scoping call rather than publishing a rate card. Ask for a written quote that separates vehicles, personnel, live monitoring and reporting. CSSG Global scopes each trip in a free 30-minute briefing and invoices in USD.

Can private security in Venezuela be armed?

Venezuelan law regulates private security closely, and armed services need a separate weapons authorization. Many reputable operators work unarmed, relying on vetted drivers, route planning, discreet vehicles and live monitoring instead. Ask any provider which authorizations it holds. CSSG Global provides unarmed services under its DIGESERVISP authorization.

How do I arrange a secure airport pickup at Maiquetía?

Book the pickup before you fly. Get the driver's name, photo, phone number and vehicle plate in advance, and meet inside the terminal rather than at the curb. Avoid unregulated airport taxis, which the U.S. advisory warns about, and keep the drive to Caracas monitored until you check in.

What does an in-country security partner do in Venezuela?

An in-country partner is the local team that turns a security plan into action: it assesses routes, hotels and sites, provides vetted drivers, follows trips live and responds on the ground if something goes wrong. CSSG Global does this from its CECOM command center in Caracas.

What is journey management, and do I need it in Venezuela?

Journey management is the planning and live supervision of every movement: route and timing approved in advance, check-ins at set points, a tracked vehicle and a command center ready to respond. In Venezuela it matters most on the Maiquetía–Caracas highway and on road travel between cities, especially at night.

How do I meet Duty of Care for employees travelling to Venezuela?

Duty of Care means taking reasonable steps to protect staff travelling for work. For Venezuela that usually means a pre-trip risk briefing, approved hotels and routes, vetted ground transport, live tracking, an emergency protocol, evacuation-ready insurance and a post-trip report. The U.S. advisory itself suggests business travellers consult a professional security organization.

Is it safe to travel to Venezuela for business in 2026?

Business travel to Venezuela is possible with proper planning. The U.S. State Department rates Venezuela Level 3, Reconsider Travel, and keeps some areas at Level 4, including the border region with Colombia. Executives should travel with a vetted local operator, pre-assessed routes and live supervision of every movement.

What is CECOM-Intel?

CECOM-Intel is the analysis unit of the CECOM command center of CSSG Global, a security company based in Caracas since 2009. It combines open-source monitoring, a human network on the ground and a live operational map to support clients' security decisions in Venezuela.

Which regions of Venezuela does CSSG Global cover?

CSSG Global's own teams cover Caracas and Miranda. In Zulia, Anzoátegui, Mérida and Lara it works through vetted local partners, coordinated live by its CECOM command center in Caracas, and it supports movements between these regions.

How is client information and dashcam footage protected?

Each client sees only its own data on the live map. Reports follow the Traffic Light Protocol, recordings are kept under restricted access and used only for the security of the service, and CSSG Global holds Cyber Essentials certification.
