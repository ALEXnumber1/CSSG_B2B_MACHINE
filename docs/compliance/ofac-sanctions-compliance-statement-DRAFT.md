## 1. Purpose

This statement describes how CSSG complies with the economic sanctions that apply to its business, in particular those administered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC). It is provided to clients and partners as part of their vendor due diligence.

## 2. Status of CSSG

- Neither CSSG nor, to its knowledge, any of its shareholders, directors or officers is listed on OFAC's Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List (SDN List) or on any other U.S., United Nations, European Union or United Kingdom sanctions list.
- CSSG is not owned 50% or more, directly or indirectly, by one or more blocked persons.
- CSSG is not owned or controlled by the Government of Venezuela, as defined in Executive Order 13884. [Confirm with counsel]

## 3. Commitments

CSSG commits to:

1. Screen clients, suppliers, subcontractors and partners against the SDN List and other applicable lists before engagement and [periodically / at least every 12 months].
2. Not provide services to, or receive payments from, blocked persons, except where authorized by an OFAC general or specific license.
3. Assess whether each engagement in Venezuela falls under an OFAC general license and keep a record of that assessment. [Confirm process with counsel]
4. Process payments only through banks and channels that comply with applicable sanctions.
5. Ensure that its U.S. entity (Company of Security and Service Global LLC) and any U.S. persons involved comply with U.S. sanctions law in every transaction.
6. Notify the affected client or partner without undue delay if CSSG becomes aware of a sanctions issue related to their engagement.
